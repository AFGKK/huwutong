🔒 互物通 (HuWuTong) Bug Bounty Program

Last updated: 2026-06-24

We take the security of our platform seriously. We welcome security researchers to help us keep HuWuTong safe for everyone. This document outlines our bug bounty program, scope, rewards, and rules.

Scope

The following properties are in scope:

  • api.huwutong.com
  • *.huwutong.com
  • SDK packages (js-sdk, python-sdk, java-sdk)
  • Mobile API endpoints

Out of Scope

  • Self-XSS that cannot be escalated
  • Social engineering attacks
  • Denial of Service attacks
  • Physical attacks on infrastructure
  • Third-party services not controlled by HuWuTong (e.g. GitHub, AWS)
  • Rate limiting bypass without demonstrated harm
  • Missing HTTP security headers without exploitability

Rewards

Rewards are based on severity and impact. All rewards are in USD.

Severity Classification Reward Range
严重 Critical Critical: $500 - $2,000+
高危 High High: $200 - $500
中危 Medium Medium: $100 - $200
低危 Low Low: $50 - $100
信息 Informational Informational: No reward (Hall of Fame only)

Rules

  1. Provide clear steps to reproduce with PoC when possible
  2. Allow reasonable time for fix (typically 90 days for critical issues)
  3. Do not access or modify user data without permission
  4. Report vulnerabilities privately — do not disclose publicly before fix
  5. Only test on your own accounts
  6. One report per vulnerability (duplicates will be marked as such)
  7. No automated scanning without prior approval

Disclosure Policy

We practice Coordinated Disclosure. After a fix is deployed, we grant permission for public disclosure after 30 days.

Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith and follow this policy.

Response Expectations

We aim to acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and provide initial assessment within 5 business days.
